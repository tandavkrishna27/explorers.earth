## Evidence limits and coverage

This review traced canonical composition, Music identity/client transport, public projection and first hosted failure families; it did not re-execute Music domain/socket suites, inspect every playback implementation, prove every live deployment route or live provider setting. Existing Music implementation is neither missing wholesale nor canonically qualified by its existence. Category-specific details are covered by categories.md; auth/media/analytics/platform content by identity-platform.md.

Future work remains future work. The current shared navigation seam and mismatched CI prerequisites block current acceptance; full Music and remaining-category parity belong to their existing tickets. Historical passing selected slices must not be promoted into milestone or release completion.
